Good to know

Pickup questions

How long does a pickup order take?

Most orders are ready in about 15 minutes. You can also schedule a pickup time later in the day.

Which location should I order from?

Orders are made at the store you choose — Eagle (115 E 2nd St) or Edwards (225 Main St, Riverwalk) — and can’t be moved to the other store once placed.

What is the glass jar deposit?

Juices, lemonades and milks come in returnable glass with a refundable deposit. Rinse and return your glass for credit on your next order.

Can I order a juice cleanse online?

Yes — see our cleanse page for packages, then sign up online at least 24 hours before your start date.
