Our story

A mountain-townjuicery.

A vibrant mountain town juicery with a mission to nurture holistic well-being and share the healing power of plants with our community.

The beginning

Two Vail Valley locals

Inner Light Juice was founded by Eagle County locals Tricia Schultheis and Keith Carrieri, who both grew up in the Vail Valley. Tricia, driven by her passion for raw food, juicing and holistic living, spent years envisioning the business; Keith, her friend and fellow plant-lover, helped bring that vision to life.

2021

The food truck

This labor of love began as a food truck serving organic cold-pressed juice and superfood smoothies to Eagle and the broader Vail Valley.

After two seasons

A home on 2nd & Broadway

After two successful food truck seasons, Inner Light Juice opened its storefront at the corner of 2nd and Broadway in Eagle.

Growing

Edwards Riverwalk

A second shop opened at the Riverwalk in Edwards, bringing Inner Light further up the valley.

Late 2024

A new chapter

Tricia followed her heart to explore new horizons, and Keith became owner — dedicated to the core values that put wellness for body, mind, spirit and planet first.

Our values

Sustainable health begins from within.

Glass, not plastic

Our juices come in glass — continuously recyclable, reusable and the safest material to drink from — with a glass return program and compostable to-go ware.

Nothing wasted

Leftover fruit and vegetables are composted or turned into other nutritious recipes, and we recycle as much as we possibly can.

Non-toxic, top to bottom

We clean with earth-friendly essential oils, baking soda and vinegar.

Food for every body

Organic, vegan, plant-based, raw, gluten-free, dairy-free and soy-free options for every allergy and preference.

Why organic

It protects farm workers, sustains family farms, builds healthy soil and keeps toxic chemicals out of our water, air and bodies — and it tastes better.

Made with intention

Our food is made to bring love and healing to everyone who enjoys it.
