Prepare for your cleanse journey

Before, during & after.

Eat mainly plants

Spend the days leading up to your cleanse eating a mostly-raw, plant-centric diet — smoothies, blended soups, salads, juices and plenty of water.

Take a break

Pause sugar, caffeine, nicotine, alcohol, processed foods, flours, grains and dairy for fewer detox symptoms like headaches and fatigue.

Set your intention

Clarify why you’re cleansing and write it down. It’s the motivation that carries you through the days ahead.

Adjustment

The first days are serious detox mode. You may feel some discomfort as your body releases what it’s been holding. Rest, and skip intense exercise.

Drink all your juice

Drink every juice each day — at a steady pace or every two hours. Each one plays a role in the process.

Hydrate

Start each day with a large glass of lemon water, and drink plenty of water and herbal tea in between.

Ease back in

Return to regular eating slowly with raw foods — fruit, vegetables, salads, soups and smoothies.

Notice the shift

Expect a surge of energy and a newfound zest for life. Recognize how light, vibrant and rejuvenated you feel.

Keep it up

Try one juice day a week, and a full cleanse every 3–6 months or with each change of season.

Cleanse questions

How do I sign up for a juice cleanse?

Sign up online or in person at our Eagle storefront. Please give us at least 24 hours’ notice before your intended start date.

When do I pick up my cleanse?

Pick up your cleanse daily during our operating hours — dates and times are arranged when you schedule. You can pick up 2–3 days at once if needed, but daily pickup keeps everything at its freshest.

How should I store my juices?

Cold-pressed juice is a raw, living product. Bring a cooler with ice when you pick up, and refrigerate your juices right away.

Which cleanse is right for me?

Awaken includes food and is the gentlest way to try a plant-based reset. Radiate is a liquid cleanse of juices and smoothies. Rise is our all-juice cleanse for a deeper reset.

How should I prepare?

In the days before, eat mainly plants and take a break from sugar, caffeine, alcohol, processed food, gluten and dairy. Set an intention and write it down. Please consult your natural healthcare provider before starting a cleanse.

Can I change or cancel my cleanse?

Changes to your pickup schedule must be made at least 24 hours in advance. All cleanse orders are final, and missed pickups can’t be refunded.
