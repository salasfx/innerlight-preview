What we do

Together we can make a difference.

We’re devoted to avoiding single-use items, lessening our impact, and safeguarding the planet, animals and future generations to the best of our ability.

We compost all of our food scraps and juice pulp.
Reusable glass jars and bottles, plus compostable to-go ware made from recycled paper.
A glass bottle and jar bring-back program.
We recycle as many materials as possible.
Stainless steel, glass, wood, bamboo, silicone and natural fibers instead of plastic wherever we can.

Glass return program

Rinse it. Return it. Save.

When you buy an item in glass, a deposit is included in the price. Bring the clean glass back with your next purchase and get the deposit off your new item.

How you can help

Three small habits.

Return your glassware

We buy back all of our glass bottles and jars to sanitize and reuse. Return rinsed glass any time for credit.

Bring your own bag

Buying more than one item or taking it to-go? Bring a reusable bag, box or carrying case.

Sort your waste

Use our onsite bins correctly: compostable to-go ware and food scraps in compost, items marked recyclable in recycling.
